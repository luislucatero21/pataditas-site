## What the data says

1. **Apple Watch intent is the largest English demand.** The queries "baby kick counter apple watch", "apple watch baby kick counter" and "can apple watch track baby kicks" add up to 26 impressions at positions 6 to 9, all landing on `/guide.html`. The guide answers the "how" but never answers "can it track kicks by itself" directly.
2. **Brand query "pataditas" sits at position 6.1 with 0 clicks.** "Pataditas" is also an everyday Spanish word, so the site competes with generic results. The structured data needs a stronger, consistent app entity: name, `sameAs` link to the App Store, and the brand first in titles.
3. **Portuguese "contador de chutes (do bebê)" ranks at positions 8 to 10.** The `/pt-br/` home page has 85 impressions but a 2.4 % CTR.
4. **The German query matches an existing section, but the heading wording differs.** People search "zählt Schluckauf als Kindsbewegung"; the heading says "Tritt".
5. **The Korean explainer is at positions 39 to 59 for "when you first feel movements" (태동 시기).** The heading uses different wording from what people search.
6. **The English explainers are new and still weak** (kick-counting position 21, timing contractions position 90). The fix is time and links rather than more copy.
7. **The site still describes version 1.3.** Version 1.5 is live on the App Store with partner sharing (Plus), baby look, the partner Live Activity and alerts, and history by day.
