# pataditasapp.com: SEO and GEO pass, November 2026

Date: 2026-11-10. Follows the 2026-10-21 audit in the app repo (`Pataditas/docs/seo-audit-2026-10-21/`).
Run the checks with `node scripts/check-site.mjs` (no dependencies).

## Findings, ranked by expected effect on installs

1. **The site had no page for two high-intent questions the app answers: timing contractions and reduced movements.**
   The App Store subtitle is "Pregnancy & Contraction Timer" and Apple Ads tested `[contador de contracciones]`, yet no page explained contraction timing. "Baby moving less" is the moment of highest anxiety; the only answer on the site was a FAQ entry in an accordion.
   Decision: two new guides in en, es and pt-BR (6 URLs).
2. **The Spanish and Portuguese explainers cited only UK and US sources, in English.** Readers in Mexico and Brazil follow different national guidance:
   - Mexico (IMSS) describes counting 10 movements in 2 hours.
   - Brazil (Caderneta da Gestante 2026) flags more than 4 hours without movement after 20 weeks.
   - The UK says not to count at all.

   Decision: rewrote both pages for their country, leading with IMSS and the Ministério da Saúde, with a country comparison table. The English page gained the same table.
3. **The safety message sat inside the quick-answer card, below the intro.** Decision: every guide now opens with a "contact your care team now, do not wait" box, written in the wording of the local authority.
4. **Home pages had a single App Store button above the long feature list and one at the very end.** On a 390 px phone the header used three rows. Decisions:
   - Added a second App Store button under pricing (all 11 languages).
   - Moved the real app screenshot up to right after the safety panel (all 11).
   - Put the phone header on two rows, with the section links on one scrollable row. The hero now starts about 40 px higher.
   - Added a "Guides" block to the en, es and pt-BR home pages.
5. **llms.txt** was out of date in three ways:
   - It said the site had only three languages.
   - It had one untracked App Store link.
   - It did not mention guidance by country.

   Decision: rewrote those sections. App Store links now carry `ct=llms`, so App Store Connect can show installs that come from AI answers.
6. **Performance:** Lighthouse mobile was already 100/100/100/100 (home LCP 1.6 s, guide 1.1 s). The only open item is the 24-hour cache on unfingerprinted assets, which is handled in a separate PR (content-hashed filenames and a 1-year immutable cache).

## Content rules followed

- Every medical statement names its source in the sentence. Each page lists its sources with the date the source shows and the date it was checked (2026-11-10).
- No reassurance and no invented statistics, reviewers or credentials. The byline is the founder, and each page says it has not been clinically reviewed.
- Practical tips that do not come from a source (what to say when you call) are labelled as ours and kept out of the FAQPage markup.
- Pataditas' 10-in-2-hours grouping and 5-1-1 alert are described as app features, never as clinical thresholds.

## What to measure in Search Console (check around 2026-12-10)

- **Indexing:** Pages → confirm all 6 new URLs are indexed. If one is not, use URL Inspection → Request indexing.
- **Performance:** filter by page.
  - New guides: impressions and average position for the query groups above. A realistic first signal is impressions, not clicks.
  - es and pt-BR explainers: compare impressions on Spanish and Portuguese queries before and after the rewrite (date comparison, 28 days).
- **Countries:** check that Mexico and Brazil grow on the es and pt-BR pages respectively. If Spain or other Spanish-speaking countries show up instead, consider an `es-MX` hreflang.
- **App Store Connect → Analytics → Campaigns:** `web_en`, `web_es`, `web_pt` and the new `llms` token (visible after 5 installs per link).

## Deliberately left out

- New guides in the 8 other languages. I could not verify those countries' national guidance at native quality.
- HowTo markup (Google no longer shows HowTo rich results). The guides use Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList and WebPage.
- A redesign. The visual language is unchanged; the edits are about order, a second CTA and the phone header.
